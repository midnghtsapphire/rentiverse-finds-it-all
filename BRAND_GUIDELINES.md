# Brand Guidelines

## Brand essence

Rentiverse is practical discovery with a sense of possibility.

It should feel:

- useful
- modern
- locally grounded
- trustworthy
- slightly aspirational without becoming vague

## Core tagline

**Rentiverse finds it all.**

## Voice

- Clear over clever
- Specific over generic
- Helpful over salesy
- Confident without sounding corporate

## Messaging pillars

1. **Access beats ownership** for occasional-use items
2. **Local discovery** makes renting convenient
3. **Curated trust** makes unfamiliar rentals feel approachable
4. **Breadth with focus** means many categories, but merchandised intentionally

## Visual direction

- Strong purple-to-pink gradients for energy and memorability
- Deep slate backgrounds for contrast and premium feel
- Rounded cards and soft shadows for friendliness
- Real photography for categories and listings, not abstract placeholders

## UI tone

- Search-first
- Category-led
- Trust-forward
- Lightweight and editorial rather than cluttered

## Avoid

- generic marketplace copy
- placeholder “coming soon” experiences in primary journeys
- overly technical language for renter-facing UI
- empty-state dead ends when live inventory is still limited
